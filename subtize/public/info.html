About

One place for every monthly service near you

Avail 100+ Subscription-Based Services for a Month — Manage & Cancel Everything from a Single Platform.

Most of us pay for a handful of local services every month: a gym, a tiffin, the laundry that picks up on Tuesdays, a car wash, a child's tuition class. Each one has its own cash or UPI payment, its own WhatsApp reminders, its own paper card, and its own awkward conversation when you want to stop. Subtize.ai replaces all of that with one account.

What you can do on Subtize.ai

  • Discover services near you. Search by typing or speaking, like "tiffin under ₹3,000 near Clement Town", and filter by distance, price, open days, plan length, service type and offers.
  • See the details before you pay. Every service shows its open days and hours, how much you can use it each month, its rules, and how many people are subscribed right now.
  • Subscribe month by month. Pick 1, 3, 6 or 12 months where offered, and pay once through the official Subtize.ai UPI QR.
  • Get verified, then get going. An admin checks your UPI Transaction ID against the payment received and activates your plan. Your digital subscription card appears in the app.
  • Track and cancel in one place. See usage against your allowance, renew, or cancel any plan from the same dashboard.

Payments

Payment information

Every subscription on Subtize.ai is paid the same way: to the official Subtize.ai UPI account, through the QR shown at checkout, and then verified by an admin.

Only pay the official Subtize.ai QR shown at checkout. The payee name is Subtize.ai Payments. Never pay a provider, their staff, or any other UPI ID or phone number for a Subtize.ai subscription, even if someone asks you to. Payments made anywhere else cannot be verified, will not activate a plan, and cannot be refunded by Subtize.ai.

How to pay

  1. Open the service, choose your plan length and add a coupon if you have one, then tap Subscribe.
  2. At checkout you see the official Subtize.ai QR. The amount and a payment reference are already filled in. Do not change either.
  3. Scan the QR with any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's app) and complete the payment. On a phone, you can also tap the button to open your UPI app directly.
  4. Copy the UPI Transaction ID (also called UTR or UPI Ref No.) from your UPI app and paste it on the payment page. You can also say it aloud using voice input.
  5. Your subscription shows Pending verification until an admin matches the payment.

Finding your UPI Transaction ID (UTR)

The UTR is a 12-digit number that identifies your payment with the bank. Each app shows it in a slightly different place:

Enter only the digits. A UTR can be used for one payment only, so re-using an old one, or someone else's, is refused automatically.

How long verification takes

An admin checks each submitted UTR against the payments received in the Subtize.ai account. Most payments are verified within a few working hours; payments submitted late at night are checked the next morning. As soon as it is verified, your plan becomes Active, your digital card appears in the app, and you get a notification.

If a payment is rejected

A payment is rejected when it cannot be matched. The most common reasons are:

  • The UTR has a typo or belongs to a different payment.
  • The amount paid differs from the amount on the QR (for example, it was edited before paying).
  • The money went to a different UPI ID or directly to the provider.
  • The UTR was already used for another subscription.

You will see the reason on the payment page. If you did pay the official QR, contact support with a screenshot of the payment and we will trace it. Money paid to the official account that cannot be matched to a plan is returned to the account it came from.

Coupons and discounts

  • Public offers are shown on each service, such as "15% off". Enter the code at checkout and the discount is shown before you pay.
  • The QR amount already includes the discount, so you pay the final figure only.
  • One coupon per checkout. Some coupons work only on particular services, need a minimum order value, cap the maximum discount, or have a limited number of uses.
  • Coupons have no cash value and cannot be applied after a payment is made.

Every active plan has a digital card with a QR code. Anyone who scans it sees whether the plan is valid, the holder's first name and last initial, the service, the plan dates, the open days and usage. Cards are personal and may not be shared or transferred.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Subtize.ai collects only what it needs to run subscriptions, verify payments and settle providers. This page explains what that is, who can see it, and how it is protected.

What we collect

From every member

  • Account details: your name, email, optional phone number and profile photo. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Subscriptions and payments: the plans you choose, amounts, coupons used, and the UPI Transaction IDs you submit for verification.
  • Usage: check-ins recorded against your plan (date, units used, an optional note).
  • Location, only when you ask: when you use "near me", your browser shares your position for that search. It is kept in your browser for 30 minutes and is not saved to your account unless you choose a preferred area in your profile.
  • Voice: voice search uses your browser's speech recognition. Subtize.ai receives only the text of what you said, never the audio.

From listers, in addition

  • Business name, address, contact details and service description.
  • Government or business registration number, address proof number and the documents you upload.
  • Bank account details, settlement UPI ID and, if you provide it, your own payout QR code.

Provider details are never shown to members

Members see service information only: name, category, area, prices, days, hours and usage policy. A lister's name, business owner, phone, email, documents and bank details are never shown on public pages or in the member app, and payment QR codes shown to members are always the official Subtize.ai QR.

What providers see about members

A lister sees the full name of members subscribed to their own services, plan dates, status and usage, which they need to deliver the service. Listers never see a member's email, phone number, address or payment details.

How sensitive data is protected

  • Bank account numbers, UPI IDs, government ID and address proof numbers are encrypted at rest.
  • They are visible only to Subtize.ai admins. Admin screens show them masked by default; revealing a full number is a deliberate action and is logged.
  • Uploaded documents are stored privately and streamed only to the lister who uploaded them and to admins, never through a public link.
  • Applicants see their own numbers masked (for example, ••••••••9012).

Card verification shows the minimum

When staff scan a subscription card, they see whether it is valid, the holder's first name and last initial only (for example, "Priya S."), the service, plan dates, open days and usage. No email, phone number or payment information is shown.

Cookies and storage

Subtize.ai sets one session cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly so page scripts cannot read it, and it is cleared when you log out. We use no advertising or third-party analytics cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps small preferences on your device, such as a referral code from a shared link or whether voice replies are on.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the lister for a service you subscribe to (as described above), with our email provider to send codes and notifications, and when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account and payment records for as long as your account is open and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by writing to support@subtize.ai.
